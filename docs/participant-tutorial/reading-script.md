# Participant tutorial — reading script

Narration and action/wait cues only. Cues are not spoken. This is the normal session flow; the operator handles exceptions.

This copy is kept in sync with the [detailed script](script.md).

## Part 1 — Welcome and orientation

“Welcome to the ELTS Project Research Study. Thank you for being a participant. Before the study begins, we'll guide you through equipment setup and practice.”

**Cue:** [CONTINUE AUTOMATICALLY] Play Part 1.1.

### Part 1.1

“We'll explain each step as you go. If you have a question or need an instruction repeated, please ask the operator.”

**Cue:** [CONTINUE AUTOMATICALLY] Leave a short pause for questions, then play Part 1.2. The operator can pause if needed.

### Part 1.2

“When you're ready, let the operator know. We'll begin with equipment setup.”

**Cue:** [WAIT — OPERATOR CONTINUE] Confirm the participant is ready, then begin equipment setup.

## Part 2 — Participant setup

“The operator will now fit you with the tracking equipment, eye tracker, and heart-rate monitor.”

**Cue:** [WAIT — OPERATOR CONTINUE] Fit all equipment before continuing to the comfort message.

### Part 2.1

“Please let the operator know if anything feels uncomfortable or needs adjusting.”

**Cue:** [CONTINUE AUTOMATICALLY] After a short pause, play the equipment-check message. Pause if an adjustment is requested.

### Part 2.2

“The operator will check the equipment before we continue. Once setup is complete, we'll move on to calibration.”

**Cue:** [WAIT — OPERATOR CONTINUE] Finish equipment checks and adjustments, then begin head-tracker calibration.

## Part 3 — Head-tracker calibration, holding position, and gun-tracker calibration

“First, we'll calibrate the head tracker. Please stand up straight and look straight ahead at the dot on the screen. Keep this position while the operator calibrates the tracker.”

**Cue:** [WAIT — OPERATOR CAPTURE] Stand upright and look at the center dot. After the complete instruction, capture the center pose and continue to the left-look cue.

### Part 3.0.1 — Look left

“Stay in the same place. Turn your head to look at the dot on the left. Keep your head upright, and hold still.”

**Cue:** [WAIT — OPERATOR CAPTURE] Complete the movement, hold still, then capture this pose after the instruction finishes.

### Part 3.0.2 — Look right

“Now turn your head to look at the dot on the right. Keep your body in the same place, and hold still.”

**Cue:** [WAIT — OPERATOR CAPTURE] Complete the movement, hold still, then capture this pose after the instruction finishes.

### Part 3.0.3 — Look up

“Face forward again, then gently look up at the top dot. Keep your body in place, and hold still.”

**Cue:** [WAIT — OPERATOR CAPTURE] Complete the movement, hold still, then capture this pose after the instruction finishes.

### Part 3.0.4 — Step left

“Face straight ahead. Step to your left until you are in front of the dot. Keep your head upright and facing forward, then hold still.”

**Cue:** [WAIT — OPERATOR CAPTURE] Complete the movement, hold still, then capture this pose after the instruction finishes.

### Part 3.0.5 — Step right

“Now step to your right until you are in front of the dot on the right. Keep facing straight ahead, then hold still.”

**Cue:** [WAIT — OPERATOR CAPTURE] Complete the movement, hold still, then capture this pose after the instruction finishes.

### Part 3.0.6 — Return to center

“Return to your starting position in the center. Look straight ahead at the center dot, and hold still while the operator checks the calibration.”

**Cue:** [WAIT — OPERATOR CAPTURE] Complete the movement, hold still, then capture this pose after the instruction finishes.

### Part 3.1 — Holding the study replica

“For this study, you'll use a replica that cannot fire. The operator will show you how to hold it with the stock against your shoulder. Please try this position, and let the operator know if it feels uncomfortable.”

**Cue:** [WAIT — OPERATOR CONTINUE] Demonstrate the stock-to-shoulder position, let the participant try it, and check comfort.

### Part 3.2 — Gun-tracker calibration

“Now we'll calibrate the gun tracker. Hold the replica with the stock against your shoulder, as the operator showed you. Look down the sights and aim at the dot on the screen. Keep this position while the operator calibrates the tracker.”

**Cue:** [WAIT — OPERATOR CONTINUE] The participant looks down the sights and aims at the screen dot. Complete and check gun-tracker calibration, then let them relax the position.

### Part 3.3 — Calibration complete

“Calibration is complete. Next, we'll show you the study interface.”

**Cue:** [CONTINUE AUTOMATICALLY] Introduce the study interface.

## Part 4 — Interface familiarization

“Let's look at the screen you'll use during the study. These are the practice targets.”

**Cue:** [CONTINUE AUTOMATICALLY] Play the aiming instruction.

### Part 4.1 — Try aiming

“Hold the replica as the operator showed you. Look down the sights and aim at the practice target.”

**Cue:** [WAIT — OPERATOR CONTINUE] Let the participant try aiming and confirm they understand.

### Part 4.2 — Try firing

“Pull the trigger to fire the gun. Try firing once.”

**Cue:** [WAIT — PARTICIPANT SHOT] Finish the instruction, then accept one recorded trigger pull. A hit is not required.

### Part 4.3 — Firing confirmed

“Well done. You've fired a shot. Next, try reloading.”

**Cue:** [CONTINUE AUTOMATICALLY] Play the complete firing confirmation, then show the reload instruction.

### Part 4.4 — Try reloading

“Use the reload action the operator showed you to refill your ammunition.”

**Cue:** [WAIT — PARTICIPANT RELOAD] Finish the instruction, then wait for an actual magazine refill.

### Part 4.5 — Reload confirmed

“Good job. Your ammunition is full again.”

**Cue:** [CONTINUE AUTOMATICALLY] Play the complete reload confirmation, then show practice readiness.

## Part 5 — Guided practice

“Now you'll have time to practice using the interface. This is practice, and your shots will not count toward the study results. Let the operator know when you're ready to begin.”

**Cue:** [WAIT — OPERATOR START] Confirm readiness, then start practice.

### Part 5.1 — Practice instruction before the timer

“Use this time to practice aiming, firing, and reloading. If you need help or another demonstration, please ask the operator.”

**Cue:** [WAIT — FULL INSTRUCTION] Play this cue with the practice timer stopped. After the entire cue, start the full configured practice duration.

### Part 5.2 — Practice complete

“Practice is complete. Let the operator know if you'd like another explanation before we continue.”

**Cue:** [WAIT — OPERATOR CONTINUE] Answer questions and confirm readiness for the ELTS demonstration.

## Part 6 — ELTS introduction and test activation

“The ELTS apparatus is directly below the middle of the screen. Its lights will be active during some of the study conditions. The operator will now demonstrate the lights briefly.”

**Cue:** [ACTION — ELTS DEMONSTRATION] With the operator ready and the downward arrow visible, cue one approved ELTS test at the end of the announcement. Wait for the demonstration to end, then play Part 7. Replaying narration does not repeat the test.

## Part 7 — Demonstration complete and readiness check

“The demonstration is complete. Please let the operator know if you have any questions before we continue.”

**Cue:** [WAIT — OPERATOR CONTINUE] Answer questions before the study overview.

## Part 8 — Procedure overview

“The study has four scenarios. You'll complete them in a randomized order.”

**Cue:** [CONTINUE AUTOMATICALLY] Explain the scenario time limits.

### Part 8.1 — Scenario time limits

“Each scenario has a time limit. The operator will prepare each one before you begin.”

**Cue:** [CONTINUE AUTOMATICALLY] Explain the breaks.

### Part 8.2 — Breaks in the sequence

“You'll have a break between scenarios and another break after the final scenario.”

**Cue:** [CONTINUE AUTOMATICALLY] Move to the first-scenario readiness message.

## Part 9 — Transition to the study

“With that, the tutorial is complete. Let the operator know when you're ready for the first scenario.”

**Cue:** [AUTOMATIC ARM] The first scenario arms after the message. The participant starts the study using Part 9.1.

### Part 9.1 — Start a prepared scenario

“When you're ready, pull the trigger to begin this scenario.”

**Cue:** [WAIT — PARTICIPANT START] The first start action begins the study. Subsequent scenarios and breaks run automatically.

## Part 10 — Breaks between scenarios

“This scenario has ended. Your break will begin shortly.”

**Cue:** [AUTOMATIC BREAK] Wait for the recording checkpoint and this message to finish. The break starts automatically.

### Part 10.1 — Break running

“Your break will begin now.”

**Cue:** [WAIT — BREAK ENDS] Run the live countdown, then play Part 10.2 automatically.

### Part 10.2 — Next scenario starts automatically

“The next scenario will start shortly.”

**Cue:** [AUTOMATIC NEXT SCENARIO] Play the transition message, then start the next scenario when tracking is valid.

## Part 11 — Completion, final break, and post-study form

“The scheduled scenarios have ended. Your final break will begin shortly.”

**Cue:** [AUTOMATIC FINAL BREAK] Wait for the final checkpoint and completion message, then start the final break.

### Part 11.1 — Final break running

“Your final break begins now. After your break, you'll complete the post-study form.”

**Cue:** [WAIT — FINAL BREAK ENDS] Run the live countdown and continue automatically to the post-study form.

### Part 11.2 — Post-study form

“Please complete the post-study form provided by the operator. If you have any questions, please ask.”

**Cue:** [WAIT — FORM COMPLETION] Provide the form and confirm successful completion, or wait for the real submission confirmation.

### Part 11.3 — Thank you

“Thank you for being a participant in the ELTS Project Research Study.”

**Cue:** [END] Hold the thank-you screen. The operator ends or resets the tutorial; do not restart automatically.
