The pictured metric kits do **not** list a 1/4-20 screw. The selected screw has 15.875 mm under-head length. The CAD reserves 3.175 mm for your additional mounting piece to retain 4.5 mm nominal thread projection. That piece is not designed or supplied here; it must bear against the recessed shoulder described in README.md. The head envelope is nominally 10.8 mm diameter and 3.2 mm tall, pending measurement of the received screw. No extra washers are specified. M6 is not interchangeable with 1/4-20 UNC.

The M2.5 kit is not needed for this variant. No new M3 screws, nuts, full wire spool, or insulation assortment is prescribed if your existing supplies meet the assembly clearances.

Verify the received pin geometry and actual fastener sizes before assembly. Follow [README.md](README.md), particularly the reversed-pin orientation and stationary-barrel solder location.
